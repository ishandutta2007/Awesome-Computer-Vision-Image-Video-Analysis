## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects



*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟



- **[OpenCV](https://github.com/opencv/opencv)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/opencv/opencv?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/opencv/opencv/stargazers)  

  **The foundational open-source computer vision library**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **80K+ GitHub stars** — **the most widely used CV library in the world** . **2,500+ optimized algorithms** for image processing, object detection, face recognition, and more . **C++, Python, Java, and JavaScript bindings** . **Runs on CPU, GPU, and mobile** . **Used in every industry** from robotics to medical imaging . **The definitively essential computer vision library** . 👁️



- **[Ultralytics YOLO](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ultralytics/ultralytics?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/stargazers)  

  **State-of-the-art object detection and image segmentation**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **35K+ GitHub stars** — **the dominant open-source object detection framework** . **YOLOv8/v11** for real-time detection, segmentation, and classification . **Pre-trained models** for 80+ COCO classes . **Easy training and deployment** with Python API . **Runs on CPU, GPU, and edge devices** . **Used in autonomous vehicles, security, and retail** . 🎯



- **[Segment Anything (SAM)](https://github.com/facebookresearch/segment-anything)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/facebookresearch/segment-anything?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/facebookresearch/segment-anything/stargazers)  

  **Foundation model for image segmentation**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **50K+ GitHub stars** — **zero-shot segmentation from prompts** . **Trained on 1B+ masks** — **the largest segmentation dataset ever** . **Works on any image without fine-tuning** . **SAM 2** extends to video . **The most important open-source segmentation model** . 🎨



- **[Transformers (Hugging Face)](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/huggingface/transformers?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/stargazers)  

  **State-of-the-art ML for vision, text, and audio**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **100K+ pretrained models** including **ViT, DETR, CLIP, and SAM** . **Unified API** for all modalities . **The de facto standard for using vision transformers** . 🤗



- **[Detectron2 (Meta)](https://github.com/facebookresearch/detectron2)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/facebookresearch/detectron2?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/facebookresearch/detectron2/stargazers)  

  **Object detection and segmentation library**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **From Meta AI Research** — **Mask R-CNN, RetinaNet, and Faster R-CNN** . **State-of-the-art detection and segmentation** . **Used for research and production** . 🏛️



- **[MMDetection](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmdetection)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/open-mmlab/mmdetection?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmdetection/stargazers)  

  **OpenMMLab detection toolbox**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **100+ detection models** — **Faster R-CNN, YOLO, DETR, and more** . **Modular design** for research . **The most comprehensive open-source detection library** . 🔧



- **[PaddleOCR](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR/stargazers)  

  **Multilingual OCR toolkit**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **80+ language support** including Chinese, English, Arabic, and Japanese . **PP-OCRv4** models for detection, recognition, and classification . **Lightweight and fast** — runs on mobile and server . **The best open-source OCR** for production . 📝



- **[Tesseract OCR](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/tesseract-ocr/tesseract?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract/stargazers)  

  **Open-source OCR engine**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **100+ language support** . **LSTM-based recognition** for high accuracy . **The oldest and most widely used open-source OCR** . **Backed by Google** . 📄



- **[DeepFace](https://github.com/serengil/deepface)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/serengil/deepface?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/serengil/deepface/stargazers)  

  **Face recognition and analysis library**, MIT licensed. **Face detection, verification, and attribute analysis** . **Age, gender, emotion, and race prediction** . **The most comprehensive open-source face analysis library** . 😊



- **[MediaPipe (Google)](https://github.com/google/mediapipe)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/google/mediapipe?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/google/mediapipe/stargazers)  

  **Cross-platform ML pipeline framework**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Face detection, hand tracking, pose estimation, and object detection** . **Real-time on mobile and edge** . **Used in Google Meet and YouTube** . **The most production-ready open-source vision pipeline** . 🖐️



- **[Albumentations](https://github.com/albumentations-team/albumentations)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/albumentations-team/albumentations?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/albumentations-team/albumentations/stargazers)  

  **Fast image augmentation library**, MIT licensed. **70+ augmentation techniques** . **Optimized for performance** . **Used by YOLO, PyTorch, and TensorFlow** . **The standard augmentation library** . 🎨



- **[FiftyOne](https://github.com/voxel51/fiftyone)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/voxel51/fiftyone?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/voxel51/fiftyone/stargazers)  

  **Open-source dataset curation and model analysis**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Visualize, curate, and analyze datasets** . **Model evaluation and debugging** . **The best open-source tool for vision dataset management** . 📊



- **[Supervision (Roboflow)](https://github.com/roboflow/supervision)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/roboflow/supervision?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/roboflow/supervision/stargazers)  

  **Computer vision utilities and tools**, MIT licensed. **Annotation, tracking, and visualization** . **Zone-based detection and counting** . **The easiest way to add vision to applications** . 👁️
